How to view it on your computer
-------------------------------
Unzip the folder, then DOUBLE-CLICK index.html. It opens in your browser.
That's the whole website running locally.


How to put it online (easiest way — about 30 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------
1. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop in your browser.
2. Drag the UNZIPPED folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a free live URL like: random-name-1234.netlify.app
4. You can connect a real domain (krengelmarketing.com) inside Netlify
   if/when you buy one.

Other free options that work the same way: vercel.com, cloudflare.com/pages.


Contact form — IMPORTANT
------------------------
The "Book a free meeting" form on contact.html sends emails through FormSubmit
to krengelmarkets@gmail.com. The very first time someone submits the form,
FormSubmit emails YOU asking to confirm the address.

Check krengelmarkets@gmail.com for that activation email and click the link.
Until you do, no submissions will reach you.
